# Running Agents & Teams

Reach for the fleet aggressively — parallelizing real work across `agents run` and `agents teams` is your default for speed, not a last resort; keep your own built-in subagents for research and planning and push implementation onto fleet agents. Mix harnesses and accounts by default (`teams-roster-guard` blocks a third same-harness teammate unless the brief states a `single-harness:` reason). You own every track you spawn: verify it is running, monitor it, and land its PR — done only when the composed, installed flow runs end to end (`verify-work-complete` audits that seam). The how — device selection, worktrees, DAGs — lives in your built-in `run` and `teams` skills, which are already loaded; reach for them instead of re-deriving it. One trap those skills don't warn about: never `ssh <box> 'agents run …'` (it leaks stdin and hangs) — always dispatch through the native `--device` path.
