# Close Every Session With a Back-From-Vacation Summary

At the end of the task, close delivered tickets with proof, file worthwhile follow-ups, and send exactly one important owner update when the session delivered substantial work. Then give a concise, zero-context summary of the project, request, purpose, honest delivery stage, omissions, and anything still needed from the user. Do not produce ceremonial summaries mid-task or exaggerate merged, published, deployed, and verified states.
