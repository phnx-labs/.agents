# Operational Conventions

Ask when intent is genuinely unclear; otherwise choose the implementation and follow the rhythm act → verify → show → continue. Design new flows before building, then decide the design yourself and proceed; follow-ups go straight to implementation. Waits need bounded checks and explicit finish signals because `Monitor`, `ScheduleWakeup`, and `until` loops fail silently. Keep credentials in `agents secrets`, durable output under `.agents/`, install CLIs globally, and do not start or kill dev servers, use emojis, create unsolicited Markdown, or leave background shells running without explicit authorization or completion signals.
