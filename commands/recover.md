---
description: Recover interrupted sessions after a crash — finish agent-doable work headlessly (alias of sessions:continue in recover mode)
---

**`/recover` is a mode of `/sessions:continue`.** It does not re-open Ghostty windows
(that is `/restore`); it finds interrupted sessions, finishes what an agent can finish,
and makes any remaining handoff one easy action.

Invoke the `sessions:continue` skill in recover mode. Arguments: recover $ARGUMENTS
